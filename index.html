About

My journey with the flute

I'm Lauren — a flautist who has spent her life chasing the warm, singing voice of the flute. From my first lessons as a child to performing with bands and orchestras today, music has always been the way I connect with people.

I perform regularly at concerts, weddings, festivals and private events, both indoors and out in the open air. Whether it's a delicate solo line or a full orchestral colour, my aim is always the same: to create a moment an audience remembers.

After a performance, the question I'm asked most is simply, "How did you learn to play like that?" — and that's exactly why I love to teach.
